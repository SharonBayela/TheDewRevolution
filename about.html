Founder & CEO

Sharon Bayela Founder & Chief Executive Officer

As the youngest child in her family, the founder of The Dew Revolution built her foundation on resilience, determination, and academic excellence, graduating with a degree in Statistics from the University of Zimbabwe. In November 2024, she launched The Dew Revolution to transform agribusiness and regional trade. To ensure the enterprise was built on solid, data-driven leadership, she made the strategic decision to pause operations and move to the United States to pursue a Master’s in Data Science at the University of Colorado Boulder. For her, blending Statistics with Data Science is the engine behind intelligent business governance. Together, these disciplines provide the predictive modeling, supply chain optimization, and quantitative precision required to turn complex market data into clear, scalable decisions—ensuring every operational shift and investment yields maximum efficiency. Her ultimate motivation stems from her own personal journey. Having navigated severe financial struggles to complete her schooling—a challenge she quietly bore while remaining steadfast—she lives by the principle to never dress your situation. That quiet endurance forged the soul of The Dew Revolution: to build a thriving business that creates sustainable employment for fellow citizens and directly funds educational opportunities for underprivileged children, empowering the next generation to rise above their circumstances.

"Where success meets greatness to further more greatness."

What guides us

Mission, vision & objectives

Our mission

The Dew Revolution exists to bring refreshing, healthy dairy products and services to the people we serve while building a sustainable livestock and value-addition business across poultry, cattle, pigs, goats, and fish, ensuring everything we produce creates real, positive change in people’s lives.

Our vision

To become a leading, multi-sector agribusiness across regional and global markets, recognized for sustainable livestock production, innovative value-addition, and an integrated supply chain that enriches communities.

Our objectives

  • Quality Dairy & Services: Deliver refreshing dairy products and services that positively impact everyday lives.
  • Sustainable Livestock Production: Expand value-addition across poultry, cattle, pigs, goats, and fish.
  • Integrated Operations: Maintain reliable agency, logistics, transport, and merchant networks from farm to market.
  • Strategic Investment & Growth: Continually reinvest capital into sustainable projects, trade, and infrastructure to drive long-term value.
